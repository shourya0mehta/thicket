Thicket

Privacy

Effective October 1, 2026

Thicket turns field recordings into species detections for farms and land stewards. It is run by Shourya Mehta as an independent project. This page covers the hosted copy of Thicket on this website.

What we collect when you sign in with Google

  • Your name, email address, profile picture link and Google account ID.
  • When your account was created and when you last signed in.

We use these only to sign you in, show who you are inside your workspace, and let workspace owners invite and manage members. We do not ask for access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

What we keep from your recordings

  • Audio is deleted as soon as an analysis finishes. Thicket does not keep your recordings unless the server is set up otherwise and you have agreed to it.
  • We keep the results: species detections, confidence scores, a spectrogram image, audio quality checks, and file details such as recording time, recorder battery and temperature, and any site location you enter.
  • Reports you generate are stored so you can download them again.

Cookies

Thicket sets one cookie to keep you signed in, plus a short-lived one during Google sign-in. There are no ads, analytics or tracking cookies.

Email

If you turn on alert emails, we send them to the address on your account. You can turn them off in Settings.

Sharing

We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. Your workspace data is visible only to the members of that workspace. The site is hosted by Render in the United States, which stores the data on our behalf. We do not use your recordings or results to train models without your separate, written permission.

Thicket's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

You can delete analyses and reports in the app at any time. To delete your account and everything tied to it, email us and we will do it within 30 days. This hosted copy runs on a free server, so stored data can be lost when the server restarts. Keep your own copy of anything important.
